How is the refund ratio determined and why does it vary?

Traditional IVF clinics charge every couple the same flat package price regardless of individual prognosis. To make fertility care fairer, the refund ratio is not fixed; it scales between 20% and 65% based on your preliminary indicator (58% for your current inputs) and is finalized following your embryologist consultation.
