I built EmbryoGuard at NEAR HACKATHON @NEARProtocol #NEARHackathon

In standard IVF care, every couple pays the same flat fee ($5k–$12k+) despite vastly different biological probabilities of success.

As a Senior Clinical Embryologist (15+ yrs ART lab experience), I built EmbryoGuard on NEAR to democratize fertility financing:
- Preliminary IVF indicator using WHO 2021 (6th Ed.) semen standards
- Dynamic outcome-based refund ratio (20%–65%) tailored to each patient
- Privacy-first NEAR.AI concierge & direct embryologist consultation
- Dynamic 3-stage NEAR Testnet escrow (NEAR Intents, HOT, Trezu, OutLayer) that automatically refunds the patient if pregnancy is negative
